Which way the screen faces

The arrow on the display points from the viewer to the aircraft. For that to work it needs to know which compass direction a person is facing while looking at the screen. On the display, “up” means “beyond the screen”, so a screen hung on a south-facing wall means the viewer faces north.

Calibrate with a plane you can see

Stand where you'd look at the screen. When you spot a plane roughly straight ahead (beyond the screen), tap it below. Low, close planes work best.

Cycling through aircraft

When something is close and low (probably the plane you're looking at) the display sticks to it instead of cycling. Avoid large values if you live next to an airport.
